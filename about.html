🌸History of Petal Bakes🌸

Petal Bakes began at Brighton university, as a group of friends - brought together by a mutual love of baking and coding, turned a shared hobby into something we're truly proud of. What started as late-night baking testing and experimenting with ideas for our own online bakery soon flourished into a passion project with real potential.

As our skills grew so did our ambition - We poured all our energy, time and resources into building Petal Bakes because we believed in our vision.

From the very beginning, we wanted to create a bakery where flavour, high-quality ingredients and intricacy were at the heart of everything we do. To make our treats more accessible, we decided to offer delivery straight to our customers' doors. Every bake is crafted with care, precision and immaculate attention to detail, always keeping your interests at the centre of our work.

Today Petal bakes stands as a testament to how collaboration, perseverance and belief in your vison can get you to places you've only ever dreamed of.

🌸OUR STORY🌸

Petal bakes was created with a simple idea: to make freshly-baked high quality treats more accessible and convenient for everyone. Inspired by the comfort and joy that baked goods bring, our bakery was founded to combine traditional baking with the ease of online ordering. We believe that everyone should be able to enjoy delicious, handcrafted pastries without having to leave the comfort of your own home.

🌸OUR MISSION🌸

Our mission is to deliver hand-crafted baked goods directly to your doorsteps with just a few clicks. You can enjoy these mouth-watering delights all baked with care, intricacy and love.
